About Me

Hi, I'm a developer who loves building things on the web. This is my little corner of the internet where I share what I'm learning and working on.

Who Am I?

I'm a curious developer who enjoys learning new technologies and building projects that solve real problems. My journey into web development started with a simple question: "How does this work?" — and it's been a fascinating ride ever since.

What I Do

  • Frontend Development: Building responsive, accessible user interfaces with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
  • Learning in Public: Sharing my learning journey through blog posts and open source projects
  • Static Sites: Creating fast, simple websites without unnecessary complexity

My Philosophy

Keep it simple. Ship fast. Learn continuously.

I believe the best tools are the ones that get out of your way. That's why this blog is built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no frameworks, no build steps, no dependencies. Just clean, maintainable code that works everywhere.
